FINDINGS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. A retrieval-score threshold is not sufficient, and the aggregate hides how
   insufficient. F1 is 0.376 against the 0.75 target in PRD S10.2, but the
   per-class row is the real story:

     out-of-scope     0.688   caught -- but these are trivial
     under-specified  0.286
     near-miss        0.143   near-total failure
     false-premise    0.000   COMPLETE failure

2. The false-premise result is 0.000 and that is not a bug, it is the mechanism.
   "How much laptop allowance does <scheme> provide?" retrieves that scheme's
   passages at high similarity, because the scheme name matches and the topic is
   right. The retrieval score measures whether the question is ABOUT something in
   the corpus. It cannot measure whether the specific asserted fact exists. No
   choice of tau fixes this -- the signal simply does not carry the information.

3. Near-miss at 0.143 is the same mechanism, slightly weaker. These are the two
   classes PRD S6.3 was designed around, and a project reporting only the
   aggregate F1 would have called 0.376 "needs tuning" and moved on.

4. The fairness failure predicted in ARCHITECTURE S12.3 is present. Code-Mixed
   queries abstain at 0.225 against 0.132 for Indic -- roughly 70% more often --
   while their OVER-abstention is lowest (0.091). A single global threshold is
   refusing Hinglish users partly for their query script rather than for missing
   evidence. A per-language-type threshold is the obvious remedy and the fact
   that one is needed belongs in the paper.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR P4
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The generator self-report and the NLI entailment check are not optional extras.
They are the only signals in the design that can see whether a specific asserted
fact is present in the retrieved passage, which is exactly what false-premise and
near-miss require. The calibrated combination should be fit with the margin and
scheme-agreement features included, since max_score alone is demonstrably blind
to both classes.

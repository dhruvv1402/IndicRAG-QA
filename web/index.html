About

A citizen asks in Hindi, in English, or — most often — in Romanized Hindi–English. The answer sits in a document written in the other language, in the other script. IndicRAG-QA finds it, cites it, or says it does not know.

40government schemes, each with an English and a Hindi article
694passages, every Hindi document through five Devanagari integrity checks
393verified questions across six query × evidence language cells
4kinds of unanswerable question, from out-of-scope to near-miss
Findings

Fusion was
the problem.

0.274→0.685

Cross-lingual Recall@5 on the sealed test split. Standard Reciprocal Rank Fusion docks a Devanagari passage for the lexical retriever's blindness to its script, and scores below the dense retriever it contains. Script-aware fusion counts only the retrievers that could have returned a passage — +0.411, p = 0.0001, no monolingual cost. Over dense retrieval alone it gains little: +0.030, significant only on code-mixed queries.

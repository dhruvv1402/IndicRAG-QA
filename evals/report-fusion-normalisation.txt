WHAT A MISSING COMPONENT SCORES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A passage only one component returned contributes 0 from the other.
  Under min-max 0 is the floor of the observed range; under z-score it
  is the mean. The imputation is not the same penalty.

  minmax    an absent dense component ranks above 0.0% of the candidates it is compared against
  zscore    an absent dense component ranks above 63.9% of the candidates it is compared against
